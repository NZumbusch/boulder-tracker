Boulder Tracker

Plan, log and understand your climbing training.

Boulder Tracker is a training companion for boulderers and climbers, made by Nathan Zumbusch. It runs on Android and in the browser, works offline, and keeps your data on your device – there is no account and no server.

PlanTraining blocks and phases, weekly sessions from templates, goals and trips.
Live sessionsGuided sessions with an interval and hangboard timer that keeps beeping in the background.
ReadinessSleep, HRV and resting heart rate with fatigue and load, as one daily score.
AnalyticsLoad and ACWR, monotony and strain, finger load, recovery trends, benchmarks and sends.
Your dataStored on your device; optional sync through a private folder in your own Google Drive.
Android extrasHome-screen widgets, launcher shortcuts, notifications and backups.
