Privacy Policy

Boulder Tracker · Last updated 3 October 2026

In short: Boulder Tracker has no accounts, no server, no ads and no analytics. Your training data stays on your device. If you switch on sync, a copy is kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives any of your data.

Who is responsible

Nathan Zumbusch · info@nathanzumbusch.de · nathanzumbusch.de

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally in the app's storage on your device (or, for the web version, in your browser's storage): training sessions and plans, exercises, notes, goals and trips, benchmark results, outdoor sends, and daily metrics you choose to log such as sleep score, heart-rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, bodyweight and pain entries. Some of this is health information; it is used only to show your own charts and readiness score in the app.

Uninstalling the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes it. Backups you export are files you create and keep yourself.

Google Drive sync (optional, Android)

Sync is off until you connect a Google account under Settings → Data & Exports → Sync. When you connect, the app asks Google for:

  • Its own app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) – a hidden folder only this app can use. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address – only to show which account is connected.

The app stores a copy of your training data in that folder (one file per device) so your phone and tablet can stay in step. The data goes directly between your device and Google; the developer has no access to it. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect at any time in the app. To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Boulder Tracker → Delete hidden app data. To remove the app's access to your account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Boulder Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide sync to you, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person.

Health Connect (optional, Android)

If you connect Health Connect under Settings → Data & Exports, the app reads the data types you allow there: resting heart rate, weight and sleep sessions. It never writes to Health Connect. From them it stores one value per day on your device (resting heart rate, bodyweight and hours asleep), which feed your charts and readiness score, the same as values you enter yourself. If you asked for past data, earlier readings are imported too.

This data stays on your device and, if you turned on sync, in your own Google Drive app folder, as described above. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used for anything but showing it to you. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect's settings; values already imported stay until you delete them.

App updates (Android)

The Android app is not distributed through an app store. It checks for newer versions by downloading a small version file from bouldertracker.nathanzumbusch.de (GitHub Pages) when it starts and at most every six hours after that, and offers an update when there is one. You can turn the automatic check off, and choose between the Stable and Testing channels, under Settings → About & Help → App updates. If you install an update, the app downloads the APK from the same address and checks it against the checksum in the version file before Android installs it. These requests carry no data from the app, but, as with any web request, GitHub sees your IP address.

Error log and feedback

If something goes wrong, the app records the error message in a log on your device (Settings → About & Help → Errors & warnings). It is never sent anywhere by the app. Under Report a bug or send feedback you can open a prefilled email or GitHub issue with your app version, device type and, only if you tick the box, the latest log entries. Nothing is sent until you send that message yourself, and the entries can mention what you were doing, so read them first.

Weather (optional)

If you set a home location, crags or trip locations, the app requests forecasts from Open-Meteo, sending the coordinates of those places, and the place names you search for when looking up a city. As with any web request, Open-Meteo also sees your IP address. No other data is sent. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information.

AI prompts

The AI features never send anything themselves: they copy a text prompt to your clipboard, and you decide whether and where to paste it. Under Settings → Data & Exports you choose which kinds of data a prompt may include; health data is off by default.

Notifications

Reminders and timer alerts are scheduled on your device by the operating system. Nothing is sent to a server.

The web version

The web version and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app does not use cookies or tracking.

Your rights

Because the developer does not receive or store your data, there is nothing held about you that could be accessed, corrected or deleted on our side – you control it in the app and in your Google account. For data held by Google, GitHub or Open-Meteo, their policies above apply. Under the GDPR you may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Questions: info@nathanzumbusch.de.

Changes
